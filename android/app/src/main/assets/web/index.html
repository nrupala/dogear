Privacy Release log

Hear your documents.

DogEar reads articles, notes and documents aloud, in your browser, and keeps your place — so you can pick up right where you stopped, on this device.

Free — 1 hour of listening every day. No account. Your documents stay on this device. Listening beyond that is a single one-time unlock, never a subscription.

This browser can’t read aloud. It has no speech voice built in, so DogEar can show your documents but not speak them. Try Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on this device — all of them can read aloud.

Choose a file from this device. .txt and .md files are free. PDF, EPUB, Word (.docx) and .html files need a DogEar licence (below). Either way, the file never leaves your device — DogEar reads it right here in your browser.

There is no size limit in DogEar: big books are welcome. The real limits are the free space on this device for the words in the file, and the time it takes to read them out of it. A large file loads in parts — you can start listening to the first part while the rest is still loading.

PDF, EPUB, Word and HTML files need a DogEar licence. Paste your licence key once — it is checked and kept only on this device.

This is the only time DogEar uses the network: our server fetches the page once, takes the readable text, and forgets the rest. See Privacy.

Your plan: 1 hour of listening free every day.

The one-time unlock is sold inside the DogEar app on Google Play. If you have a licence key, enter it here — it unlocks this web version the same way, unlimited listening included.

DogEar is a small, independent app. If it earns its keep for you, a tip helps it keep being built — entirely optional, and it changes nothing about your listening.

              About DogEar

              DogEar reads your documents aloud — articles, notes, books, files — in your device’s own voices, and keeps your place so you can pick up where you stopped. It is built by 2351485 ALBERTA INC., a small independent maker.

              How DogEar is paid for

              Every day includes 1 hour of listening free (60 minutes). Listening is counted by the document, not the clock: hearing a passage again costs nothing, and a document you have started can always be finished — DogEar never stops in the middle. Beyond the free hour, one payment of US$24.99 unlocks unlimited listening for good. There is no subscription and there never will be. A DogEar licence key, if you have one, unlocks this device the same way.

              Your documents and privacy

              There is no DogEar account. Your documents and your place in them live only on this device, and nothing about your reading is tracked. The full story is on the Privacy page.

              Two-way voice (your choice of model, an optional download)

              If you unlock DogEar, you can also talk to it. Speech recognition needs a model, and you pick which one: the reading screen offers a shelf of Whisper models — Tiny, Base, Small, Medium and Large — each shown with its size before you download anything. Pick a model and it downloads once, together with the shared runtime that runs it, and then works on this device. Nothing is bundled with the app. Each model carries its own licence, shown on the shelf before download, and the model you choose is the one that runs. With a model in place you can talk to the reader — pause, resume, go back — and ask questions about the document you have open. Answers are read straight from the document’s own words; DogEar does not make answers up, and if the document doesn’t answer a question, it says so. Your voice is recognized on this device and is never uploaded — there is no transcription service behind it. A downloaded model stays on this device, works without a connection, and can be removed from the reading screen at any time; you can keep more than one and switch between them.

              Ask for an improvement

              Tell us what to fix or add. Requests people send shape what DogEar becomes next, and we read every one. Sending a request is the one time the app sends something you typed to us: exactly the words below, kept so we can read them. Nothing else travels with it — no document, no reading history.

              Report a problem

              Something broken? Tell us here. Pressing send files your report as an issue in DogEar’s issue list, where we work through them — and this page answers with the issue number, so you can see it was filed. The report carries exactly what is listed under “What will be sent” below: your words, and nothing else — no document, no conversation, no reading history.
