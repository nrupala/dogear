Privacy, in plain words

DogEar was built so there is almost nothing to promise: your documents never reach us in the first place.

Your documents stay on this device

Documents you paste or upload are kept in this browser’s local storage, on this device only. They are not uploaded, not synced, and not backed up anywhere by DogEar. If you clear your browser’s site data, or remove a document in the app, it is gone.

That includes PDF, EPUB and Word files: they are opened and read by tools built into the app itself, right here on your device — the file is never sent anywhere to be converted. A DogEar licence key, if you use one, is likewise checked by the app on this device and kept only here; checking it makes no network call.

Things are kept per device and per browser. A document you add here does not appear on your other devices — there is no DogEar account to sign in with.

The one network exception: fetching a link

When — and only when — you use From a link, our server fetches that page once, extracts the readable text, and returns it to your browser. The fetched page is processed in memory and is not stored. Like any web request, the fetch itself passes through our hosting provider’s standard edge logs (time, address requested, response code); we do not build profiles from it and there are no analytics or trackers in this app.

The second exception: an improvement request you choose to send

The About page has a form for asking us to fix or add something. If — and only if — you write a request there and press Send, the words you typed (and an email address, if you add one so we can answer) are sent to us and stored so we can read them. Nothing else travels with it: no document, no reading history, no identifier for you or your device beyond what our hosting provider’s standard edge logs record for any web request, as above. If you never use the form, nothing is ever sent.

The third exception: a problem report you choose to send

The About page also has a “Report a problem” form. If — and only if — you write a report there and press Send, the words you typed are filed as an issue in DogEar’s issue list, together with the product name, the app version, and whether you are on the web app or the Android app — the form lists exactly that before you send, and the app answers with the issue number. An email address travels only if you add one so we can answer. Nothing else goes with it: no document, no conversation, no reading history. If you never use the form, nothing is ever sent, and the app sends nothing on its own — there is no automatic error reporting and no telemetry.

Purchases in the Play app

In the Google Play app, the one-time unlock and tips are sold by Google Play; Google handles the payment and tells the app the purchase happened. The app keeps the fact of the unlock on your device, and may present the purchase receipt to our server to be checked — the receipt names the product bought, nothing about your documents or reading. Listening is counted on your device only (the app keeps a daily total there so the free hour can be applied); that count is never sent anywhere.

Two-way voice and the voice models

If you unlock DogEar, you can choose to download an optional voice model — speech-recognition models are offered as a choice (Tiny, Base, Small, Medium, Large), each one a download you pick, under that model’s own licence, which is named on the shelf before you download it. Nothing is bundled with the app. A model downloads once from us, as data files, together with the shared runtime that runs it; nothing about you travels with that download beyond the request itself, and the size is shown before you fetch it. From then on, when you tap Talk and speak, your voice is recognized on this device, by the model on this device: the recording exists only in the device’s memory until it has been turned into words, no audio is uploaded, and there is no transcription service anywhere in the design — we could not listen to you if we wanted to. Voice commands steer the reader on the device, and answers to questions about your document are quotations from that document, found on the device — your questions and documents are not uploaded for answering either. Each model can be removed from the reading screen at any time, which deletes that model’s files from this device; reading works exactly as it did before any model arrived.

Voices

Reading aloud uses your browser’s speech voices. DogEar prefers voices that live on your device. Some browsers also offer network voices, which send the text being read to the browser maker’s servers to produce the speech — that is your browser’s doing, and your browser’s voice settings decide which voices exist. If keeping text fully on-device matters for a document, pick an on-device voice in your browser or system settings.

What we do not do

No accounts. No advertising. No analytics or tracking in this app. No selling or sharing of anything — there is nothing of yours here to share.
